## Textbook
- Martin J. Osborne, *An Introduction to Game Theory* ([website](https://www.economics.utoronto.ca/osborne/igt/index.html))
- Also useful: Tadelis, *Game Theory: An Introduction*; Gibbons, *Game Theory for Applied Economists*

## Course outline (Osborne chapters)
- **Introduction:** Ch. 1 (reading Ch. 17 beforehand may help)
- **Complete information games**
  - Strategic form: examples 2.1–2.5; dominated strategies 2.9, 12; Nash equilibrium 2.6–2.8, 4, 3
  - Extensive form: backward induction & SPE 5–7, 16.1; repeated games 14, 15.1–15.3
- **Incomplete information games**
  - Bayesian games: 9
  - Dynamic games of incomplete information: 10
